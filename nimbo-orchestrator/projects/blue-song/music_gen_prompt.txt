Gentle, melodic children's lullaby-pop for toddlers.
Mood: warm, calm, curious, soothing — never frantic or shouty.
Tempo: slow and steady, about 70-80 BPM, in a major key.
Instrumentation: soft acoustic guitar, gentle piano, light glockenspiel/bells, warm pads, very light hand percussion (no harsh drums).
Vocals: a single warm, friendly voice; simple, repetitive melody a young child can sing along to.
Length: about 180 seconds (~3:00).
Theme: learning the color blue — repeat the key word "blue" often as a memory aid.
Keep it uncluttered and premium: one or two motifs, lots of space, soft dynamics.
